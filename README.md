## About

The **IEEE Computational Intelligence Society (CIS) Student Chapter at MJCET** is a community of students interested in **computational intelligence** and **emerging technologies**, bringing together individuals from diverse engineering backgrounds to learn and innovate. The chapter emphasizes **hands-on learning** through projects, workshops, competitions, and expert talks, helping members bridge the gap between theory and practical application. Through collaboration and IEEE's global network, the chapter fosters professional growth while encouraging students to turn **ideas into real-world impact**.

| 20+ Research Papers | 100+ Technical Workshops | 1500+ Followers |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Published in recognized journals and conferences | Successfully conducted across diverse domains | A growing community across leading social platforms |

## Website Features

| Section | Description |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Hero section, Faculty Advisor spotlight, and chapter overview |
| **Team** | Governing body and department-wise member cards with batch year filters |
| **Activities** | Workshops, hackathons, seminars, donation drives, and press coverage |
| **Blogs** | Dynamic blog posts powered by Sanity CMS |
| **Contact** | Get in touch with the chapter |

### UI Highlights

- **Blazing fast** — Vite-powered build pipeline
- **Rich animations** — GSAP scroll-triggered effects + Framer Motion page transitions
- **Custom cursor** — Fluid cursor with hover interactions
- **Fully responsive** — Mobile-first layout across all screen sizes
- **Premium dark aesthetic** — Deep dark UI with cyan accents
- **Team Member Modals** — Interactive cards with member detail pop-ups
- **Headless CMS** — Blog content managed live via Sanity Studio
